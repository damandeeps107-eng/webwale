FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS • TRANSPARENT ANSWERS

Everything you need to know before starting a build with Web Wale.

No corporate jargon or hidden fine print. Here are honest, direct answers to common questions about our timeline, pricing, ownership, and performance guarantees.

Q: How long does a custom web build take from start to finish?

A: Most custom builds take between 2 to 4 weeks. We follow a strict 4-step launch framework (Discovery → Bespoke Design → High-Performance Code → Launch & Indexing). You receive regular interactive staging links throughout the process.

Q: Do I own 100% of my site, domain, and code after launch?

A: Absolutely yes. Upon final launch, you own every single asset, graphic, domain name, and line of code. We believe in 100% client ownership with zero vendor lock-in.

Q: How does pricing work? Are there any hidden monthly fees?

A: We work on transparent flat-rate quotes. You pay a 50% deposit to initiate design and the remaining 50% upon final launch approval. There are zero surprise monthly fees unless you choose our optional Care & Growth hosting plan.

Q: How do you guarantee sub-second load speeds and 99+ Core Web Vitals?

A: We hand-code our frontends with clean HTML5, Next.js, and lightweight CSS instead of bloated WordPress templates or 30+ third-party plugins. We compress all images into WebP formats and leverage global CDN caching.

Q: Will my site rank on Google Search?

A: Yes. Every website we launch comes pre-configured with technical on-page SEO, Schema.org JSON-LD rich snippets, XML sitemap generation, and immediate Google Search Console indexing.

Q: What if I need updates or changes after the site goes live?

A: We offer our zero-stress Care & Growth plan for monthly content updates, security patches, and traffic analytics. Alternatively, you can reach out for quick ad-hoc updates anytime.
